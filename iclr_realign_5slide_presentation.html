Red Team: choose 1,000 images to minimize CKA

Dogs remove the easy contrast.

We restricted the candidate pool to 125 dog and canid synsets, then ran proxy-model divergence scoring and simulated annealing with incremental Gram updates to select the final 1,000 images.

Within dogs, the variation that matters is pose, texture, background, crop, and scene. CNNs, ViTs, CLIP models, and masked autoencoders organize those axes differently.

Report CKA as a conditional measurement.

The Red result transferred to hidden evaluation: 0.547 vs the 0.479 baseline. The proxy overestimated, but the direction held.

The broader point: alignment scores depend on what the images ask models to agree about. At minimum, report broad-category and fine-grained stimulus conditions separately.

Likely questions

Is this metric hacking?

Yes, in the challenge sense. That is the point: CKA is conditional on stimulus composition.

Are dogs a true single class?

No. They are a fine-grained superclass. The goal is to reduce broad category contrast, not remove every semantic difference.

Does this mean CKA is useless?

No. It means CKA needs stimulus controls and careful interpretation.

What about proxy mismatch?

11 proxies at 224px vs hidden models at 160-1024px. The direction transferred; the magnitude did not.
